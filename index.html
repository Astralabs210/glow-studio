About GLOW STUDIO

MORE THAN A SALON. A BEAUTY EXPERIENCE.

GLOW STUDIO is a calm, considered space created for people who value beauty as a personal ritual. We believe that great beauty work begins with listening — to your preferences, your lifestyle and the version of yourself you want to express.

Our approach blends modern technique with a gentle, attentive touch. Every service is tailored, unhurried and designed to leave you feeling confident, refreshed and entirely yourself.

Whether you are preparing for a special occasion or simply investing in everyday care, we are here to make your moment feel special.
